About

Spell Sling is a small card game I worked on over the summer of 2026. Its tries to evoke the idea that the players are wizards in a duel, planning out their next few moves to hopefully outplay each other.

Card Frame Evolution

  • Tried to keep relatively big text for readability and because I wanted to use a more handwritten font.
  • Accent on border added to insinuate the divide between the art and text box.
  • Divides and different formatting used to distinguish the different parts of the effect text.

Card Layout

Togglable components and automatic formating allow easy changes to card layout.

I then use a tool to convert excel data into all the card images without having to change them manually.

Quick Reference Cards

Initial rules cards made for teaching. Basic game flow on the front with important, more forgettable, rules having different colors and icons to draw attention. Back side has reminders of how specific cards work.
